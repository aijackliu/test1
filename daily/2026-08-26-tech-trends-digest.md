Privacy and provenance are becoming a louder counter-theme as AI features move into everyday software. Reverse-engineering work showing Microsoft Paint and Photos embedding server-issued GUIDs into locally generated images has become a major Hacker News discussion, reinforcing a broader push toward transparency, control, and skepticism about hidden AI-era tracking or watermarking behavior.
